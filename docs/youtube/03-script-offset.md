## 00:06–00:25 WHAT OFFSET DOES

Screen: original polyline + a copy offset 10 units away, perfectly parallel.

Lines, polylines, circles, arcs — anything. This is how you build wall
thickness, spacing, and machine outlines in real CAD."

## 00:25–01:15 STEP BY STEP — LINE

Screen: draw a line; type OFFSET.

VO: "Draw a line. Now type OFFSET — or just O, Enter. First it asks for the
distance. I'll type 10 and press Enter."

[Screen: type 10, Enter]

VO: "Now click the line, and click the side you want the copy on. Boom — a
parallel line, exactly 10 units away. Click again on the same side for another
copy, or change the distance any time."

## 01:15–01:50 BUILDING A WALL

Screen: existing rectangle/room outline; OFFSET it outward by 5 to make a
wall; hatch between.

VO: "Here's the wall trick. I have a room outline. OFFSET it outward by 5 —
the offset follows the whole shape, corners and all. Now I have two outlines
that define a wall, exactly 5 thick. That's how floor plans are built in
practice."

[Screen: offset the rectangle, then HATCH between the two outlines]

VO: "Add a hatch between them and you have a proper wall section. Hatching is
a future video — for now, look how clean the geometry is."

## 01:50–02:25 CONCENTRIC CIRCLES

Screen: circle, OFFSET it inward/outward repeatedly.

VO: "Circles work the same way — offset a circle inward for a bolt pattern
ring, outward for a flange. Every copy is a true circle, perfectly centered.
No math, no guesswork."
